# P1 statistics -- INTERIM, CORRECTED COSTS

> **Interim — corrected costs.** Computed from the completed P1 arms of the R2C batch while P3/P5/P4/P2' are still running. P1 is complete (20/20 seeds x 4 arms), so these numbers will not change unless P1 is re-run; "interim" refers to the overall R2C results package.

- Corrected data: `results/r2/corrected/hardening/hardening_results.csv` (audit fixes #8 slippage units, #9 regime look-ahead applied).
- Old data (**defective costs**, pre-fix): `results/hardening/hardening_results.csv`, from `results/hardening/stats_report.md` and `paired_test_report.md`.
- Fold 2 test, n=20 seeds/arm. alpha=0.05; MDE at 80% power (two-sided, exact noncentral t via statsmodels) using each comparison's own observed SD.
- `turnover` = mean daily turnover; `cost/NAV` = sum over days of transaction cost / NAV.
- Generated by `analyze_p1_interim.py`. No training was run. Fold 4 not touched.

## 4. Power statement

Independent comparisons (n=20/group): MDE at 80% power ranges **0.095 to 0.132** across the 8 comparison x metric cells. Paired canonical-vs-untrained (n=20 pairs): MDE **0.097 to 0.111**. An observed difference smaller than its own MDE that is non-significant is uninformative about a smaller real effect -- not evidence of zero effect.

## 5. Comparability with old values

- Old net-return and cost/NAV figures were computed with the defective slippage units (#8) and regime features carrying look-ahead (#9); they are **not** valid results, shown only to quantify the correction's effect.
- Trained arms were re-trained under corrected costs, so new−old differences in trained arms mix the accounting fix with a different learned policy. The untrained arm (no training) is the closest to a pure accounting/observation comparison, but old-vs-new untrained init identity was not verified here.
- Old paired results cover seeds 6-20 only; the new n=20 paired result has no old counterpart.
